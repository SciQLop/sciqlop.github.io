title: Workshlop 2026
---

The **3rd SciQLop Workshlop** took place on **September 15–17, 2026** at the Jussieu campus, Paris. It was an **in-person only** event, limited to **~50 participants**.

We showed what was new — real-time collaborative catalog editing, enriched virtual products, isolated
workspaces, a community App Store, and deeper Jupyter integration. Participants brought their own science:
results obtained with SciQLop, and data-analysis challenges it could help with.

## Program

| Day | Content |
|-----|---------|
| Tuesday Sept 15 (½ day) | SciQLop overview and new features |
| Wednesday Sept 16 (full day) | Science presentations — results with SciQLop, and studies that could benefit from it |
| Thursday Sept 17 (full day) | Hands-on sessions — tutorials and real use cases |

A **conference dinner** was held on Wednesday evening.

## Venue

Campus Pierre et Marie Curie (Jussieu), Place Jussieu, 75005 Paris.
