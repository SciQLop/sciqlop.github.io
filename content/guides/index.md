---
title: User guide
---
One guide per analysis tool. Each one shows the tool on a real event and covers its options. They assume you know
the [tutorials](/tutorials/), [[virtual products]] in particular.

- [[guides/Graphic primitives|Graphic primitives]]: lines, text, arrows, bands and images on top of the data.
- [[guides/Annotation layers|Annotation layers]]: marks computed from the data, recomputed as you pan and zoom.
- [[guides/DSP toolbox|DSP toolbox]]: filtering, resampling, spectrograms and background subtraction on Speasy variables.
- [[guides/2D histograms|2D histograms]]: density maps of one quantity against another.
